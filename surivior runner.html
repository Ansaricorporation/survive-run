How to Play

Controls

  • Space or ↑ — Jump (press again for double jump)
  • ↓ or S — Slide under barriers
  • 1 — Activate Phase (50 coins)
  • 2 — Activate Super Speed (100 coins)
  • 3 — Activate Ghost (150 coins)
  • Esc or P — Pause

Survival

The cursed monsters chase you endlessly. Dodge obstacles to maintain your speed — hitting them stuns you and lets the monsters close in. If a monster touches you, the run ends instantly and U LOSE TO DEMONS! Don't let them catch you.

Abilities (60s Duration, 5s Cooldown, Stackable)

  • Phase — Pass through all obstacles unharmed. The world bends around you.
  • Super Speed — Double movement speed and 50% higher jumps. Outrun everything.
  • Ghost — Monsters cannot perceive you. Walk freely among the shadows.

Progression

Level up by covering distance! Every 800m traveled grants 1 Level. Unlock new characters and accessories automatically as you reach new levels. Earn +1000 Coins for every 1 Kilometer (1000m) you survive.
